Who we are

A studio that treatsa home as somethingto be lived in.

Anaya is a residential interior practice working across Bengaluru. We take a home from measured survey to final styling under one contract, with our own production facility behind every piece of joinery we specify.

That matters because most of what goes wrong in an interiors project goes wrong at the handover between trades. When design, manufacture and installation sit with one team, the drawing that was approved is the thing that gets built.

We work to a fixed, itemised price. If something cannot be done within your budget, we say so at the quotation stage rather than halfway through.

What we do

Seven services,one accountable team

Each can be taken on its own, or combined into a single turnkey contract with one handover date.

All services
  • 01 Modular Kitchen Ergonomic layouts, soft-close hardware and surfaces chosen to survive daily cooking.
  • 02 Bedroom Restful palettes, considered storage and lighting layered for evening use.
  • 03 Living Room Rooms arranged for how people actually sit, talk and host.
  • 04 TV Units Media walls that hide the cabling and still look composed when switched off.
  • 05 Wardrobes Internal planning done around a real wardrobe, not a generic one.
  • 06 False Ceiling Ceilings that carry light and services without lowering the room.
  • 07 Complete Interiors One contract, one team, one handover date for the whole home.
