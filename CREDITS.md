# Image credits

All photography on this site is the studio's own project work, supplied by the
client. It is not stock, and no attribution to a third party is required.

The source files live outside the published site in `images by client/` as
full size PNGs. Each one is resized to a maximum long edge of 1600px and
re-encoded as JPEG at quality 0.82 before being written to `assets/img/`. That
takes the set from roughly 38MB of PNG to about 4MB of JPEG with no visible
loss at the sizes the pages actually display.

Every image passes through a shared warm grade in CSS so that photographs taken
on different days, in different light, read as one set.

## Stock images still in use

Two slots are not the studio's own work:

| Slot | Source | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| before-kitchen.jpg | Pexels | No pre-work photograph was supplied |
| before-living.jpg | Pexels | No pre-work photograph was supplied |

These are the two "before" frames in the comparison slider on the Projects
page. The client set contained no pre-work photography and no matched pairs,
so stock stands in for now. Both are from Pexels, whose licence permits
commercial use without attribution; the specific photo ids were not recorded
when they were first added.

The page carries a visible note saying these frames are stock. Keep that note
until the frames are replaced, or remove the section. A before and after
comparison is a claim about a specific project, and it only holds up when both
frames are the same room shot from the same position.

## Not used

Five supplied photographs were left out: an exterior view of the apartment
block and its pool, a bathroom, a 268px thumbnail too small to publish, a
bedroom with clothes and bags left on the bed, and a duplicate frame. The
remaining unused files are alternate angles of rooms already represented.
